Research Assistant / Hong Kong Metropolitan University

Mingyang Wang

王明洋

I work on UAV systems for search and rescue, with an interest in connecting sound perception, artificial intelligence and mission planning.

I am a research assistant in Dr. Yalin Liu’s group at Hong Kong Metropolitan University, which I joined in March 2025. My work includes victim sound detection and localization, and user-triggered UAV dispatching for mountain search missions.

I received my BSc (Honours) in Computer Science from HKMU in June 2026, following earlier studies in optoelectronics. Alongside my research, I co-founded GuTelligence, a smart-toilet health-monitoring project.
